# Desktop adapters without the peripheral role (2026-10-19)

On a desktop (Windows, later macOS) the callee and the viewer are the advertising GATT server, and callers and sources connect to them as central (PROTOCOL "Experimental iPhone compact discovery" and "Experimental rotating discovery IDs", viewer requests). A PC whose Bluetooth adapter cannot take the peripheral role, or whose advertisement fails to start, can therefore only place outgoing calls. **Decision:** the app probes the central and peripheral roles, confirms the peripheral role by actually starting the advertisement, and shows Watch, Go live, receiving calls and being visible to contacts as unavailable with a plain reason ("This PC's Bluetooth adapter can't advertise, so phones can't connect to it. Calling a phone still works. A Bluetooth 5 USB adapter that supports peripheral mode fixes this."). No wire change and no weaker fallback (no Classic, no fixed IDs, no cleartext). A viewer-initiated join (the viewer dials the source's connectable advert) would change admission keying (RD3), token grace and the link-opening roles. It needs its own design and review and is not planned until a measured share of real PCs lack the peripheral role.

**Design decision 2026-10-19:** a Windows PC becomes visible to phones by the filterable PC announcement (amendment A2 below: one fixed service-data UUID with a short payload, no name on air, and only from an address that an observer proves rotates). The PC may use its camera and microphone for short tests (camera covered or pointed away, a quiet room, nothing kept after the measurement).
