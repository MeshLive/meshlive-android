# Design UP: a call that survives a failed 2M upgrade (draft 2026-11-11, review 1 2026-11-12, not built)

**Problem.** A call that starts on LE 1M (the Experimental 1M-first start) must ask for 2M when 1M cannot carry it, but on some pairs a 2M link stalls at the link layer and dies within seconds: both calls from the A15s to the ASUS that asked for 2M were lost 4.6 s later. Holding 1M saves those calls but costs healthy pairs whose call does not fit 1M (the S22 calling the A15s over LE CoC: a freeze and +350 ms for most of the call). Asking the same dead link to go back to 1M cannot work: a link that no longer carries packets cannot carry the PHY procedure either.

**Idea.** Extend Design SR (in-call link renewal, on by default for direct calls) with a renewal that is armed before the risky step instead of after the stall. Before a first-1M call asks for 2M, the asker sends an end-to-end `RENEW_PREARM(ticket, reason=phy_upgrade)` on the healthy link and waits for `RENEW_ARMED`; only then does it ask for 2M. If the link is lost within the early-loss window after the request (5 s), neither side ends the call: the peer arms the Design SR renewal advert for that ticket, and the asker connects to it on LE 1M, remembers the peer as bad on 2M by its authenticated node, and binds the call to the new link with Design SR's `RENEW_BIND`. Without `RENEW_ARMED` (an older peer, or no answer within 1 s) the call stays on 1M, as the hold does today.

**Rules carried over from Design SR, unchanged:** hop-local, direct calls only; the renewal bit in both end-to-end hellos; single-use ticket bound to the call ID and both identities; renewal advert with a session-derived UUID, no announcement and a fresh address; pre-proof before any handshake byte; the old link's pinned peer identity; at most one renewal per 60 s and two per call; a hard bound after which the call ends as link lost.

**What changes:** a second trigger (link loss within 5 s of a 2M request that was pre-armed), and the asker dials the renewal on 1M. The upgrade can then be asked for right after the first picture whenever the call's planned load of both directions exceeds 1M, instead of waiting for congestion.

**Open questions for review:** whether the peer may keep the renewal advert armed for the whole 5 s window or only once it sees the link lost (privacy: an armed advert is visible); whether a link-layer supervision timeout of 5 s lets the loss be seen in time; how the call's media backlog from the gap is bounded (Design SR rule 6).

**Review 1 (2026-11-12, independent): build only with changes, as a gated experiment.** A 5 s window can miss a plausible loss and a new client may still ride the old link, so the renewal needs an agreed end-to-end lease, progress seen independently of blocked reads and writes, and proof of a fresh 1M link (or the call ends within a real bound); the feature is negotiated separately from Design SR so an older peer, a stale reply or an early advert sighting can never drop a good call; the shared renewal limits are reserved on both sides before the upgrade (no slot, no risky upgrade); stale media is bounded and the codec restarts cleanly. The renewal advert stays available from ARMED for the whole agreed lease, stops at bind, cancel, expiry or hang-up, and the radio-exposure budget is amended openly. A simpler first change for the one measured bad pair: a narrow, authenticated exclusion of 2M for that pair, transport and role through the existing per-peer memory, with the early-upgrade trigger corrected for healthy pairs; Design UP then covers the first meeting with an unknown bad pair.
