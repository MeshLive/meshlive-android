# Design QL: recognition keys (presence leases) during contact verification (2026-10-19; revision 2 2026-10-21)

**Problem.** Two people scan each other's codes and then cannot call each other from Contacts: with rotating IDs on, a contact is found by its rotating call ID, which only a presence lease lets this phone compute, and leases move only inside an answered call. So after a mutual scan the first call must go through Nearby (generated names only), which defeats the point of scanning. This design moves the existing lease exchange forward into the BVMID2 verification session. It reverses Design G review row F8, which rejected this for the first release.

**Rule (wire: PROTOCOL "Scope of the session opened with a code").** In a `K_qr` session, after its completed end-to-end handshake, each side may send at most one `BDX1` `RECOGNITION_OFFER` and at most one `PRESENCE_LEASE`, under exactly the Phase 2 rules of a call (`PresenceSession`, `PresenceEligibility`, the send-time re-check, the durable delivery record of `PresenceVault.deliverLease`, and storage only after both OFFERs to a peer that is VERIFIED right now). There is no new record, field, kind or version. `BDX1` `OPENING` and `IDENTITY_REVEAL`, a second OFFER or LEASE, and every other content type still close the session as a scope violation.

**When a side starts the exchange (the "answered" moment of RD6). The scanner always goes first.** The scanner starts when its user answers "Is this X's screen you scanned?" with yes and the shield was stored (never on the tap alone). The displayer never offers at the commit. It answers the scanner's OFFER, and only if all of these hold: the code was committed by this connection; its own user confirmed this scanner with yes **during the current visit of the Add contact screen** (the "I scanned them" of this visit); the scanner answered with its real identity (no pseudonym); and the link peer equals the endpoint peer (direct, no relay). Eligibility (verified contact, switch on, persistent vault) is decided when the side starts, not when the session is built, and is re-checked when each record is sent; the displayer's per-visit rule is re-checked inside the vault's lease step. An OFFER that arrives before this side started is recorded, not acted on, exactly as in a call. Why RD6 holds: RD6 forbids an OFFER that would tell an unanswered caller that this phone holds its fingerprint. Here the scanner offers only after its user confirmed whose screen it scanned, and the displayer answers only a person its user confirmed minutes earlier on the same screen. A holder of a leaked or photographed code gets nothing, even when it is an older verified contact (review QL-R1), and the scanner refuses any key other than the code's.

**Order of two scans.** First scan (B scans A): B offers after confirming; A has not confirmed B in this visit, so A does not answer, and no lease moves. Second scan (A scans B): A offers after confirming; B confirmed A in the first scan, so B answers with its OFFER and lease; A sends its lease; both store. Leases therefore move in both directions in the second session, and Contacts can call from then on. If the user confirms after the session ended, nothing is lost: the leases follow at the next answered call as before (a UI hint "Scan their code again to finish" is a follow-up).

**Session end.** A displayer that will not answer ends the session 2 s after the handshake, as before. A scanner waits at most 20 s for its user's answer and a displayer at most 20 s for the scanner's OFFER (the call endpoint serves one connection, so a longer wait would refuse the reverse scan and real calls). A side in the exchange ends it 2 s after it has sent or withheld its lease and received the peer's, never past the fixed 60 s. A "No", a cancelled dialog or a failed shield write ends it at once. A reuse alert on the displayed code withholds every lease not yet sent for the rest of the visit. The 300 s code life, the 3-attempt cap and the single-use reservation are unchanged. A Forget after a lease delivery starts a new generation as for any contact (Phase 2 item 6).

**Scope on both phones.** Both phones enforce the session scope (the scanner had none before, harmless for 2 s, not for 20). A `BDX1` record is checked by kind before it is dispatched: only kinds 2 and 3, once each per direction, and only after a committed code (displayer) or a reached scan (scanner); `OPENING` and `IDENTITY_REVEAL` close the session, so a reveal can never swap the identity of a verification session.

**Not changed.** No ring, recent-call entry, label persistence or message exchange. BVMID1 static codes open no session, so they carry no lease (with `bvmid2` off the behaviour is unchanged). **Default (2026-10-19):** `bvmid2` is on by default (design decision Q-QL1), so this exchange is the normal way two people who meet become callable from Contacts. No automatic connection from a recognition hit (RV3).

**Alternatives considered.** (A, chosen) the exchange above, with no wire change. (B) a new "verified each other" control record and one exchange after both scans: it needs a new record type and gives no extra security, because each side already decides from its own verified store. (C) keep F8 and tell the user to call once through Nearby: this is the bug being fixed.

**Review (fresh-context, 2026-10-19): go with changes, all adopted.** QL-R1 (high) the displayer offered to any eligible contact at commit, so an older contact that won the race for the code got a fresh lease: per-visit confirmation rule and reuse-alert withholding. QL-R2 eligibility was frozen when the session was built: decided at start, re-checked at send. QL-R3 scope per `BDX1` kind on both phones, uncommitted displayer refuses every `BDX1`. QL-R4 scanner first (old scanners never receive a record). QL-R5 20 s waits; cancel counts as No. QL-R9 direct link and real identity required. Residuals: QL-R6 an eligible peer that never sends its lease keeps the other phone busy until the wait ends and can leave a one-way lease (the next call completes it); QL-R7 an older displayer closes on the scanner's OFFER after the commit: one counted violation, verification already done, no lease, no capability bit needed; QL-R8 a passive observer can tell a lease-carrying verification session by its length and the ~914-byte records, so it learns the two phones became mutual contacts (as for a call, Phase 1b item 5); no padding.

**Tests.** JVM: the scope allows exactly OFFER and LEASE once each and closes on OPENING, REVEAL, a duplicate or before negotiation; the order of two scans (no lease after the first, both after the second, scanner first); a scanner that never confirms stores nothing; a withheld OFFER settles the session; the per-visit rule refuses an older contact and everything after a reuse alert; the Meet text changes only after a stored lease. Phone (`test_bvmid2` on, both phones on one frozen build): scan both ways, call from Contacts at once, call again after a 15-minute epoch rotation; this also runs the BVMID2 phone check N5.

**Phone result (2026-10-19, build a58be974, two pairs).** After a mutual scan, Contacts calls found the contact by its rotating ID and connected in both directions at once and after a 15-minute epoch change: 8/8 calls (M31/S22 ACTIVE in 6.6-7.5 s; ASUS/A15s 6.6-8.2 s, A15s as caller 12.5-17.6 s, the known slow first connect of that pair). The first scan moved no lease and the second moved both, as designed, with no scope violations. The test found one bug the review had not: the scanner still armed the RV15 OPENING wait for the version 5 advert it dialled, so the displayer's OFFER closed the session; fixed (PROTOCOL Leases bullet).

## Revision 2 (2026-10-21): consent at the scan, a plain result and Call now (design only, not built; Experimental, default off)

**Why a revision.** The user asked again (2026-10-21) for a new contact to be callable from Contacts right after a QR add, without a first call from Nearby. The exchange above already does that and passed on phones, so the remaining complaint comes from the cases it leaves behind and from what the screens say. This revision answers the six points of the request; it adds no record, field, kind, version or capability bit.

1. **The verification connection today.** "Show my code" draws a `BVMID2` code (fingerprint, fresh 32-byte `K_qr`, current RDI; 300 s, single use, 3 attempts, `FLAG_SECURE`) and advertises a version 5 call offer. The scanner finds that RDI and connects as for a call (BMH3 R_FIRST with the contact tag keyed by `HKDF(K_qr)`, `expectedPeer` = the code's node ID), then runs the end-to-end handshake on the same connection; the code is reserved at the link handshake and consumed at the end-to-end one. Authenticated: both real keys (the scanner refuses a pseudonym and any key other than the code's; the displayer learns the scanner's key from the session). Exchanged: the scanner's optional signed name label, then (QL) one OFFER and one LEASE per direction; nothing else (PROTOCOL "Scope of the session opened with a code"). The shield is set only after the user answers "Is this X's screen you scanned?"; each person scans once, so a pair runs two sessions (`AndroidPrivateVideoCall`, `Bvmid2.kt`, `VerifyContactActivity`, `MeetBridge`).
2. **The lease today.** `PresenceVault` holds this phone's 28-29 independent daily keys and, per contact, the switch "Can see when I'm nearby" (default on, design decision Q4), `deliveredGeneration` and the contact's lease (`identity-verification/presence-leases.bin`, Keystore-sealed). `deliverLease` re-checks verified, switch and persistent store and records the delivery durably before a 914-byte `PRESENCE_LEASE` leaves; the receiver stores only after OFFERs in both directions and only from a peer VERIFIED right now (log `rotid_presence lease_stored`). The rule is **mutual**: a side whose switch is off sends no OFFER, so neither side stores a lease. Turning the switch off after a delivery, Forget, a reset and "Reset presence key" start a new generation (the "new private ID" of `verify_visible_nearby_description`). **Gap:** for a new contact the switch appears only on the contact's page after the add, so the first lease leaves under the default before the person has seen the choice.
3. **New: consent at the scan.** The confirmation dialog gets one checkbox, "Let <name>'s phone recognise yours nearby" (default on, as Q4), with the line "You can then call each other from Contacts. Turn off to stay unrecognised; you can still call from Nearby." The answer is written with `setVisible` for that contact **after** the shield is stored and **before** `scanConfirmed(yes)`; if the write fails the side withholds its OFFER (fails closed). Each person confirms once (as scanner), so each person's own dialog governs both the OFFER they send as scanner and the answer they give as displayer later in the visit. Unchecking for a contact that never received a lease changes nothing on the wire and starts no generation. Declining stays mutual (no one-way watching, Q4).
4. **New: a plain result.** The Add contact status after both scans: (a) lease stored in this visit: "<name> added. You can call them from Contacts when they're nearby." (b) this phone declined: "<name> added. <name> can't see when you're nearby, and your phones won't recognise each other. Call from Nearby." (c) no lease and this phone did not decline: "<name> added, but your phones didn't finish recognising each other. Scan each other's codes again, or call once from Nearby." Text (c) does not guess why: a peer that declined, a late answer, an older build and a dropped link look the same, and that ambiguity protects the person who declined. (d) after one scan: the existing text plus "Show them your code so you can call each other from Contacts." `meet_each_other` stops promising "after your next call".
5. **New: Call now.** Shown with text (a) only, on both phones, for 60 s or until the screen closes. It starts an ordinary Contacts voice call (new connection, normal contact handshake and RV15 opening), never on the verification link: that link's scope forbids `INVITE` so that a code holder never gets a contact-privileged link, and an exception "after leases moved" would be a second rule to get right for about 7 s saved (measured QL connects 6.6-8.2 s). If the peer is not reachable for calls (the code screen advertised only for its own life) the normal "not found nearby yet" dialog follows.
6. **Switch.** `ql_consent_ui` (Experimental, default off; DEBUG `test_ql_consent_ui`): off keeps today's dialog and texts. It enters SWITCHES with the code.

**Security.** *Binding:* a lease rides only a session whose end-to-end key equals the code's fingerprint (scanner) or that was committed on the displayed code by a scanner this phone's user confirmed in this visit (displayer), so it is bound to the verified identity and to the person whose screen was scanned. *Replay:* a replayed msg1 is closed by the pinned-first-message rule and cannot complete a handshake without the private key. *MITM or relayed code:* a phone that copies the code within its 300 s life can complete the scanner side but receives nothing, because the displayer gives a lease only to a phone whose own code its user scanned and confirmed. *A malicious verifier* (a real person you confirmed) learns your fingerprint, a 4-week window of day keys (it recognises your rotating IDs until the generation changes), your claimed name and that you accepted: the same as after a call. *Revocation:* switch off, Forget or "Reset presence key" starts a new generation; old keys still resolve the days they cover (Phase 2 residual). *Downgrade and interop:* no wire change; an older scanner never offers, an older displayer closes on the OFFER after the commit (QL-R7), a 0.2.0-beta phone cannot read `BVMID2`, a `BVMID1` code opens no session; all keep today's behaviour. *On air:* nothing; leases travel only as end-to-end content, adverts carry only the RDI. A passive observer can still tell a lease-carrying session by its length (QL-R8).

**Review 1 (self, skeptical, 2026-10-21); every change is already in the text above.**

| # | Risk | Change |
|---|---|---|
| QL2-R1 | A default-on checkbox is still a default; a hurried user consents without reading. | Kept default on (Q4 is a design decision); the line says what it does and that Nearby still works. Open question Q-QL2a. |
| QL2-R2 | An explicit "declined" record would tell the other side for certain, which can be unsafe for the person who declined. | No decline record; text (c) names no reason. |
| QL2-R3 | A checkbox written after the OFFER could leak a lease the user refused. | Written before `scanConfirmed(yes)`; a failed write withholds. |
| QL2-R4 | Call now over the verification link would widen the scope rule. | A new ordinary connection. |
| QL2-R5 | Both people press Call now and two calls cross. | Phone gate (a) requires exactly one call; if the existing role tie-break fails it, Call now is shown only on the phone that scanned last. |
| QL2-R6 | A later switch-off revokes every contact (one shared key window), and those contacts cannot find this phone from Contacts until a mutual session: the original symptom, caused by revocation. | Not solvable without several IDs on air. The description line gains "Your other contacts will find you again after your next call or scan with them." Open question Q-QL2b. |
| QL2-R7 | Leases last 4 weeks and renew only at a mutual session; contacts who do not meet for 4 weeks lose Contacts calling. | Out of scope (no wire change); the SCAN_AGAIN hint covers it. Open question Q-QL2c. |
| QL2-R8 | iPhone builds without QL leave iPhone contacts on the old path. | The iOS port of Design QL is still pending; an Android-iPhone pair must show text (c), never a false (a). |
| QL2-R9 | A user who answers the dialog after the 20 s wait is told "next call", which is false. | Text (c) says scan again. |

**Tests.** JVM: checkbox off writes `setVisible(false)` before `scanConfirmed`, the scanner sends no OFFER and the same phone as displayer withholds; a failed `setVisible` withholds; unchecking with no prior delivery starts no generation; the status text for each outcome (a)-(d); Call now appears only after a lease stored in this visit and is never sent on the verification session (the scope still closes on `INVITE`); switch off reproduces today's texts. Phone (one frozen APK, `test_ql_consent_ui` on, every eligible pair, the same gates for all): (a) both allow: lease stored both ways, Contacts call ACTIVE in both directions at once and after an epoch change, Call now ACTIVE from each side, both pressed together leaves exactly one call; (b) one declines: no `lease_stored` on either phone, no generation change, texts (b) and (c), a Nearby call still works; (c) confirmation after more than 20 s: text (c), a rescan then stores; (d) peer on build a58be974 in both roles: shield set, no lease, at most one counted QL-R7 violation, no crash; (e) radio capture during (a): no lease bytes outside the encrypted session. A Nearby fallback in (a) is a fail.

**Open questions.** Q-QL2a: keep the checkbox default on (recommended: matches Q4 and the goal) or default off (stronger consent, most adds stay uncallable). Q-QL2b: accept the revocation cascade with a clear line (recommended) or design per-contact recognition (several IDs on air or trial matching: more airtime and tracking surface). Q-QL2c: accept the 4-week expiry (recommended) or longer leases (longer exposure after a phone is lost).
